---

## How to use this workbook

| Activity type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wet Lab** | Hands-on laboratory work with living material or chemicals |
| **Demonstration** | Instructor-performed; students observe and record |
| **Simulation** | Model-based exercise (software or worksheet) |
| **Computational Exercise** | Student analyses data in Python/spreadsheet |
| **Case Study** | Paper exercise on real documented scenarios |

**This course is predominantly non-wet-lab by design:** environmental risk assessment is practised through data, models, and structured reasoning. No biosafety-critical materials are used anywhere in the program.

## General safety statement

No living GMOs, pathogenic material, chemical hazards, or fieldwork are involved in any practical. The professional-safety rules that *do* apply everywhere: (1) never present simulated data as real; (2) never state conclusions your evidence cannot carry; (3) cite comparators explicitly; (4) carry uncertainty honestly.

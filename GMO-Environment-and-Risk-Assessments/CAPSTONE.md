# Capstone Project — GMO Environmental Risk Assessment

**The course's synthesis exercise · Full template and rubric in [Lab 12](LAB/Lab-12-Capstone-Environmental-Risk-Assessment.md)**
*Instructor: Dr. Saira Azam, CEMB, University of the Punjab, Lahore.*

## The assignment

Select a **real, documented GMO** and construct a complete **conceptual environmental risk assessment** for a **specified receiving environment**. The capstone demonstrates every course competency in one artifact.

## Required sections (report structure)

1. GMO description & genetic modification
2. Intended trait & mechanism
3. Receiving environment specification
4. Problem formulation
5. Hazard identification (table)
6. Exposure pathways (figure required)
7. Potential ecological effects
8. Non-target effects
9. Gene-flow assessment
10. Resistance considerations (if applicable)
11. Food/feed considerations (if applicable)
12. Uncertainty analysis
13. Risk characterization (table + matrix)
14. Risk management (instrument per risk)
15. Monitoring plan (baseline, indicators, triggers)
16. Final evidence-based conclusion

## Deliverables

- **Report** (Lab 12 template, ~20–30 pages) with executive summary in lay language
- **Presentation**: 15 minutes, 10–14 slides; audience includes a "regulator" and a "reviewer"
- **Viva defense**: questions drawn from the [viva bank](ASSESSMENT/Viva-Questions.md)

## Assessment rubric (100 points)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Problem formulation & scope | 10 |
| Hazard identification quality | 15 |
| Quantitative backbone (≥4 elements) | 20 |
| Uncertainty treatment | 15 |
| Risk characterization & matrix | 10 |
| Management & monitoring integration | 15 |
| Communication products | 10 |
| Objectivity & science/policy discipline | 5 |

**Non-negotiables:** no invented data; uncertainty carried, not hidden; every risk statement paired with an instrument and trigger; the science/policy boundary respected; conclusions falsifiable (state what monitoring result would change them).

## Suggested GMO choices (verify with primary sources)

Bt cotton (Cry1Ac+Cry2Ab) in Punjab · Bt maize in a European context · HT soybean in South America · Golden Rice in the Philippines · Bt brinjal in Bangladesh · virus-resistant papaya · a gene-edited (SDN-1) crop under two regulatory regimes · a GM biocontrol microorganism (advanced) · stacked-trait maize (advanced).

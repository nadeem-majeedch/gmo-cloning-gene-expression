## Datasets reproduce the documented answers (verified by running `analysis_demo.py`)

- **Bulk RNA-seq:** exactly **18 genes** at FDR<0.05 & |log2FC|>1; top hits form the **5-gene module** (g011–g015, log2FC ≈ −4.2, KO-down); up-regulated set g016–g028 (log2FC ≈ +2.7)
- **Single-cell:** three clusters of **~170 / 220 / 210 cells**, each with a single dominant marker (hbbe1 / pax6a / myod1); transition subcluster (low-myod1) recoverable at k=4
- **Spatial:** region markers recover as top-1 per region (**shha**→notochord, **myod1**→somites, **pax6a**→neural tube); **edge_gene** gradient Pearson **r ≈ −0.89**
- **qPCR time course:** *myod1* rises 12→18 hpf (ΔΔCt ≈ −2.9 → FC ≈ 7.5× vs 10 hpf); *shha* stable; *pax6a* rise-and-plateau
- **Dual luciferase:** motif-mutant ≈ 80% reduction vs WT promoter; enhancer orientation-invariant

## Limitations (explicit)

1. **Simulated data only** — pedagogical, biologically plausible, deterministic; not citable as experimental data.
2. **Regulatory statuses change** — re-verify Golden Rice approvals, gene-edited crop statuses, clinical-editing approvals before lecturing on them.
3. **Commercial systems described at arm's length** — Gateway covered conceptually (per spec).
4. **No operational wet-lab/animal protocols** — by design; institutions must supply approved SOPs for any hands-on component.
5. **Diagrams are schematic teaching illustrations**, not publication-grade figures or real micrographs.
6. **Citation transcription** — REFERENCES.md avoids DOIs deliberately; confirm via PubMed/publisher before academic citation.
7. **Spatial dataset resolution** — 20×10 spot grid for teaching feasibility; real platforms are higher-plex/higher-resolution.
8. **Deployment verified live** — the URL above was confirmed after the workflow run; if the repository is later renamed or made private, the URL changes accordingly and Pages must be re-checked.
9. **PDF links inside website pages** point to copies under `docs/assets/` (required for Pages deployment); repository-root `PDF/` files remain canonical and regenerate from the same scripts.

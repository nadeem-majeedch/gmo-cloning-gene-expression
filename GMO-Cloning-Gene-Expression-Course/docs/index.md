# GMO, Cloning Techniques & Developmental and Spatial Gene Expression

### A complete Molecular Biology learning module

A rigorous, self-contained teaching module for BS/MS students in Molecular Biology, Biotechnology,
Bioinformatics, Genetics, Microbiology and Biomedical Sciences. Learn the theory, follow the molecular
mechanisms, work through the simulated data, complete the labs, and revise with the cheat sheet,
FAQs and assessments.

## How to use this site

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-map-marker-path: **Follow the learning path** - work through the modules in order;
  every module ends with practice questions and a link to the next one.
- :material-flask: **Do the labs** - nine exercises (simulation / computational / demonstration) with
  instructor keys in the [lab workbook](guide/workbook.md).
- :material-database: **Analyze the data** - all [datasets](downloads/datasets.md) are simulated,
  deterministic and clearly labeled.
- :material-check-decagram: **Assess yourself** - [MCQs](assessment/MCQs.md), short and long questions,
  viva, case studies and an instructor [answer key](assessment/Answer-Key.md).

</div>

# About this course

**GMO, Cloning Techniques & Developmental and Spatial Gene Expression** is a complete, university-level teaching module assembled for direct classroom use by professors and self-study by students. The repository contains the full Markdown source, a 107-slide lecture deck, five printable PDFs, nine labs with instructor keys, 19 simulated datasets and a complete assessment package.

## Instructor

**Dr. Saira Azam** — Assistant Professor  
Centre of Excellence in Molecular Biology, University of the Punjab, Lahore.

## Safety and responsible use

This is an educational package. Wet-lab activities are framed as conceptual workflows or SOP-governed teaching procedures: institutions must supply approved protocols, biosafety approval and supervision for any hands-on component. No operational animal, viral-vector or plant-manipulation parameters are included by design.

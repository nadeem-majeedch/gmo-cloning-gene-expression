# Assessment — 40 Viva-Voce Questions

**Format:** rapid oral exam; student answers in 1–3 sentences; examiner probes with follow-ups (listed where useful). **Answer guidance:** [Answer-Key.md](../assessment/Answer-Key.md)

---

### Cloning & vectors (1–10)

1. What is the difference between an insert and a vector?
2. Name three functions carried by a plasmid backbone.
3. Why does copy number matter when expressing a toxic gene?
4. What ends does EcoRI leave, and why are they called "sticky"?
5. Why can BamHI and BglII ends ligate to each other?
6. What does T4 DNA ligase need as a cofactor?
7. Why is a 3:1 insert:vector ratio a starting point rather than a law?
8. In blue-white screening, what does a white colony tell you — and what doesn't it?
9. Why is colony PCR with one vector + one insert primer orientation-specific?
10. What is a shuttle vector — give a concrete use case.

### Techniques (11–16)

11. Why does Gibson Assembly not need restriction sites?
12. What enzyme property makes Golden Gate overhangs "programmable"?
13. Why does a Golden Gate reaction become more product-specific as it proceeds?
14. What is an entry clone in Gateway-style logic?
15. When would blunt-end cloning be your only option?
16. Why must high-fidelity PCR products be A-tailed before TA cloning?

### GMO & CRISPR (17–26)

17. Define transgenic organism in one sentence.
18. Why is Dolly the sheep not a transgenic animal?
19. What is a binary vector system in plant transformation?
20. Why does totipotency make plant engineering conceptually simpler?
21. What is a PAM and why can't Cas9 cut without one?
22. NHEJ vs HDR — one-line definitions.
23. Why do knock-ins require more design than knockouts?
24. What is mosaicism and when does it arise?
25. What is the difference between CRISPRi and CRISPRa?
26. Why do base editors avoid the HDR-efficiency problem?

### Reporters & expression (27–33)

27. Why does GFP not need a substrate?
28. When would you pick lacZ over GFP?
29. What does the Renilla control correct for in a luciferase assay?
30. What is a destabilized reporter and what question does it answer?
31. Why can a reporter pattern disagree with endogenous ISH?
32. What is a Kozak sequence?
33. What changes when you swap a constitutive promoter for a tissue-specific one?

### Developmental & spatial expression (34–40)

34. Define differential gene expression.
35. What is a morphogen gradient — give one real example.
36. What are Hox genes and what does colinearity mean?
37. Gradient vs domain — how would you distinguish them quantitatively?
38. Why does scRNA-seq lose spatial information, and which method restores it?
39. What does a sense-probe control measure in ISH?
40. Why do statistics on ΔCt rather than fold change in qPCR?

---

**Examiner follow-up bank (use to probe depth):**

- "Give an example." · "What would happen if that component were missing?" · "Which control would detect that failure?" · "State the limitation of that method." · "How would you validate that claim independently?"

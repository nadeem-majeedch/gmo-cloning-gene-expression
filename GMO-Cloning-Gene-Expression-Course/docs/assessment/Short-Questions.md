# Assessment — 30 Short-Answer Questions

**Expected length:** 3–6 sentences each · **Model answers:** [Answer-Key.md](../assessment/Answer-Key.md)

---

1. Define recombinant DNA technology and name the landmark 1973 experiment that established it.
2. Distinguish transgenic from cisgenic organisms with one example each.
3. Why is an origin of replication essential in a plasmid vector?
4. State the role of three components of an expression cassette.
5. What is a multiple cloning site and why is it useful?
6. Define directional cloning and name the design feature that achieves it.
7. Why is vector dephosphorylation used in ligation setups?
8. Give one advantage and one limitation of TA cloning.
9. What makes Type IIS enzymes central to Golden Gate assembly?
10. Describe the three enzymatic activities in a Gibson Assembly reaction.
11. Explain the difference between selection and screening with one example each.
12. How does blue-white screening distinguish empty from insert-carrying colonies?
13. Why is a colony on a selective plate only a *candidate* clone?
14. What does Sanger sequencing add beyond a correct restriction digest?
15. Distinguish constitutive, inducible, and tissue-specific promoters.
16. Why does GFP require no exogenous substrate?
17. When is luciferase preferable to GFP as a reporter, and why?
18. Define morphogen and state the French-flag model in one sentence.
19. What is colinearity in Hox gene organization?
20. Name two signaling pathways that pattern the vertebrate neural tube and their sources.
21. Define cell determination and give an experimental criterion for it.
22. Distinguish spatial from temporal gene expression with a concrete example of each.
23. Why does bulk RNA-seq average away spatial information?
24. What is the purpose of the sense probe in ISH?
25. Why must reference genes be validated for each qPCR experiment?
26. State the ΔΔCt formula and the assumption behind it.
27. What is mosaicism in F0 genome editing and why does it matter?
28. Give one example each of NHEJ- and HDR-based outcomes in CRISPR editing.
29. Why is dropout a caution when interpreting scRNA-seq absence calls?
30. State the four layers of GMO risk reasoning in the correct order.

---

**Grading guide:** each question is scored for (1) correct concept, (2) precision of terminology, (3) example or criterion where demanded. Model answers in [Answer-Key.md](../assessment/Answer-Key.md).

# Assessment — 20 Long-Answer Questions

**Expected length:** 1–2 pages with diagrams where useful · **Model answer outlines:** [Answer-Key.md](../assessment/Answer-Key.md)

---

**Molecular cloning & vectors**

1. Describe the complete molecular-cloning workflow from target-gene identification to sequence-confirmed construct. For each stage, state purpose, common errors, and validation.
2. Compare restriction–ligation, Gibson, Golden Gate, and Gateway cloning. Include mechanism, strengths, limitations, and a justified scenario for each.
3. Explain plasmid vector architecture in detail: for each component (ori, marker, MCS, promoter, terminator, reporter), state its function and the consequence of its absence.
4. Design (on paper) an expression construct for a zebrafish developmental gene with a C-terminal His₆ tag: elements, order, cloning strategy, screening, and validation plan.
5. Discuss expression-vector design choices that control expression level, timing, and tissue specificity, with one concrete example of each change and its predicted effect.

**GMO generation & CRISPR**

6. Compare the conceptual workflows for generating genetically modified bacteria, plants, and animals: DNA delivery, selection, regeneration/development, and validation. Include one landmark example per kingdom.
7. Explain Agrobacterium-mediated plant transformation at the molecular level: Ti plasmid, T-DNA borders, vir functions, binary vectors, and why totipotency matters.
8. Describe the CRISPR–Cas9 mechanism from guide-RNA design through target recognition to repair-pathway choice, and explain how knockout vs knock-in outcomes arise.
9. Compare base editing and prime editing with nuclease editing: mechanisms, what edits each achieves, and the trade-offs.
10. Critically compare traditional transgenesis with genome editing: what each is best for, and why many modern projects use both. Include off-target and mosaicism considerations.

**Developmental & spatial gene expression**

11. Explain differential gene expression as the basis of development. Include cell fate, determination, differentiation, potency, and the master-regulator concept with its caveat.
12. Describe the morphogen concept using Shh in the vertebrate neural tube: source, gradient, thresholds, fates, and why duration of exposure matters.
13. Outline the Drosophila segmentation GRN hierarchy (maternal → gap → pair-rule → segment-polarity → Hox), explaining how each level refines spatial precision.
14. Compare enhancers, silencers, promoters, and operators: location, activity logic, and experimental demonstration methods for each.
15. Explain gene-regulatory-network motifs (positive feedback, mutual repression, feed-forward, lateral inhibition) and the developmental behavior each produces, with one example each.

**Methods & data analysis**

16. Compare RT-qPCR, RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, and spatial transcriptomics: question answered, workflow, output, and the interpretation each supports. Include a decision guide for choosing among them.
17. Describe the in-situ-hybridization workflow end to end, including probe logic (antisense/sense), controls, and how you would interpret a domain vs a gradient from ISH data.
18. A qPCR experiment reports an 8-fold increase of *myod1* at 18 hpf. Design the full validation and extension plan: statistical treatment, spatial validation, protein-level check, and functional follow-up.
19. Explain the single-cell RNA-seq computational workflow (QC → normalization → PCA → clustering → annotation → trajectory) and the biological claim each step licenses.
20. Discuss reporter-based expression analysis: construct types (promoter-, enhancer-, fusion-reporter), controls, interpretation limits, and how reporter data should be validated against endogenous expression.

---

**Grading guide (per question, 10 pts):** scientific accuracy (4) · completeness vs the prompt (3) · correct use of examples/evidence (2) · clarity and structure incl. diagrams (1). Model outlines in [Answer-Key.md](../assessment/Answer-Key.md).
